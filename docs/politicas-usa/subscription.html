Subscription & Automatic Renewal Terms

1. How it works

When you subscribe to Club Dog or Club Cat, you agree to a monthly subscription that renews automatically until you cancel. Each month you receive a themed box with products picked for your pet's profile.

  • Your first charge is made at checkout.
  • Renewals are charged on the 1st of each month to the payment method you selected (PayPal).
  • Boxes ship between the 8th and the 10th of each month.

2. Price

Prices are in US dollars. Shipping to the contiguous United States is included. Sales tax is added at checkout where applicable. If your subscription price changes, we'll email you at least 30 days before the new price applies, and you can cancel before then.

3. Pause, skip or cancel anytime

You can pause, skip a month, change your pet's size or life stage, or cancel online at any time from your account. No phone calls, no cancellation fees.

  • Changes made by the last day of the month apply to the next month's box.
  • If you cancel after the renewal charge on the 1st, you'll still receive that month's box and your subscription will end afterwards.

4. Prepaid plans and gifts

Prepaid 1-, 3- and 6-month plans do not renew automatically. If you cancel a prepaid plan, we'll refund the prorated value of the boxes that haven't shipped yet.

5. Failed payments

If a renewal charge fails, we'll retry it for up to 7 days and let you know by email. If it still fails, your subscription will be paused until you update your payment method.

6. Confirmation

After you subscribe, we'll email you a confirmation with these terms, your renewal date and instructions to cancel.
