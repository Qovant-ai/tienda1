Club Terms

1. Plans

Pawlet Club plans (Club Dog and Club Cat) are prepaid for 1, 3 or 6 months and charged once at checkout. They do not renew automatically; we'll email you before your last box so you can order again if you'd like.

2. Shipping schedule

  • You get one box per month for the length of your plan.
  • Boxes ship between the 8th and the 10th of each month. Orders placed by the last day of the month get the next month's box.
  • Shipping to the contiguous United States is included.

3. Price

Prices are in US dollars. Sales tax is added at checkout where applicable.

4. Changes and cancellations

  • To change your pet's size or life stage, or the shipping address, email us by the last day of the month and we'll apply it to the next box.
  • You can cancel a plan anytime. We'll refund the prorated value of the boxes that haven't shipped yet.

5. Gifts

Gift the Club plans follow the same rules. The boxes ship to the recipient's address, and refunds go to the person who paid.

6. Box contents

Each box includes 5 to 6 products picked around a monthly theme and your pet's profile. Products may vary from the photos.
