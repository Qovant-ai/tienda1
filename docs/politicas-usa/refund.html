Refund Policy

1. Happiness guarantee

If something in your order arrives damaged, defective or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 30 days of delivery through our contact form with your order number and a photo. We'll send a replacement or issue a refund to your original payment method.

2. Returns

Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Email us first and we'll send you instructions. For hygiene and safety reasons, treats and snacks can only be returned unopened.

3. Club plans

Club plans are prepaid and don't renew automatically. You can cancel anytime by email and we'll refund the boxes that haven't shipped yet. Details are in our Club Terms.

4. Refund timing

Approved refunds are processed within 5 business days. Your bank or PayPal may take a few more days to show the credit.
