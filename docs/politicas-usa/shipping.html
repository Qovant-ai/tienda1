Shipping Policy

1. Where we ship

We currently ship to the contiguous United States (48 states and Washington, D.C.). We don't ship to Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories, APO/FPO addresses or outside the US yet.

2. Cost

  • Free standard shipping on orders of $30 or more. Every Club box, gift and limited edition qualifies.
  • Orders under $30: $5.99 standard shipping.
  • "Add to Your Box" extras ordered with your box ship in the same package.

Items from our Shop may ship from a different warehouse than your Club box, so an order can arrive in more than one package.

3. Timing

  • Subscriptions: boxes ship between the 8th and the 10th of each month.
  • One-time orders: ship within 2 business days (pre-orders ship on the date shown on the product page).
  • Delivery: usually 3–7 business days after shipping.

4. Tracking

We'll email you a tracking number as soon as your order ships.

5. Lost or wrong address

Please double-check your address at checkout. If a package is returned because of an incorrect address, we'll work with you to reship it; reshipping costs may apply. If your package shows as delivered but you can't find it, contact us within 7 days and we'll help.
