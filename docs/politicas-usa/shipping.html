Shipping Policy

1. Where we ship

We currently ship to the contiguous United States (48 states and Washington, D.C.). We don't ship to Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories, APO/FPO addresses or outside the US yet.

2. Cost

  • Free standard shipping on orders of $30 or more. Club boxes and gifts always ship free.
  • Orders under $30: $5.99 standard shipping.

3. Timing

  • Shop items: ship from US warehouses within 1–3 business days.
  • Delivery: usually 3–7 business days after shipping.
  • Club boxes: ship between the 8th and the 10th of each month. Orders placed by the last day of the month get the next month's box.

Our products ship from different US warehouses, so an order can arrive in more than one package.

4. Tracking

We'll email you a tracking number for every package as soon as it ships.

5. Lost package or wrong address

Please double-check your address at checkout. If a package is returned because of an incorrect address, we'll work with you to reship it; reshipping costs may apply. If your package shows as delivered but you can't find it, contact us within 7 days and we'll help.
