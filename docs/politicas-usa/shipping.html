Shipping Policy

1. Where we ship

We currently ship to the contiguous United States (48 states and Washington, D.C.). We don’t ship to Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories, APO/FPO addresses or outside the US yet.

2. Cost

Free standard shipping on every order, with no minimum.

3. Timing

  • Orders ship from US warehouses within 1–3 business days.
  • Delivery usually takes 4–8 business days from the day you order.

Our gifts ship from different US warehouses, so an order can arrive in more than one package.

4. Tracking

We’ll email you a tracking number for every package as soon as it ships.

5. Lost package or wrong address

Please double-check the address at checkout, especially when shipping a gift to someone else. If a package is returned because of an incorrect address, we’ll work with you to reship it; reshipping costs may apply. If your package shows as delivered but you can’t find it, contact us within 7 days and we’ll help.
